Last year I had the good fortune to acquire one of the limited-edition physical CDs of Jason Riddell's *[Some Grief](https://jasonriddell.bandcamp.com/album/some-grief-ep)* EP. It was a subtly bombastic release with a lot of confidence, openness, and vulnerability. That made for a really interesting mix, sparking mental comparisons to [Travis](https://travisonline.com/), [Stereophonics](https://www.stereophonics.com/), and [Supergrass](https://www.supergrass.com/). It's a great release and well worth a listen. It made me sad and comfortable in a satisfyingly calm way that I rather enjoyed.

As such, when I saw that Riddell had a new EP out, it's fair to say that my expectations were high. *[Throwback To Another Dream](https://jasonriddell.bandcamp.com/album/throwback-to-another-dream-ep)* is a smaller release than *Some Grief*; both in runtime and intent. The 'Jason Riddell' sound I loved in *Some Grief* is still very much present, but it feels less like being at a gig and more like being invited into a cozy living room for a chat. The music feels even more personal, and it drew me in right from the get-go with a piano solo piece simply titled 'Instrumental.' I imagined myself crossing paths with Jason on a rainy urban street, being invited to his flat for a cup of tea and a biscuit.

I can't claim to know Riddell's intentions for any of these songs, but for me *Instrumental* seems to evoke an unexpected happiness in a moment of shadow, and the embrace of warmth that follows.

The EP then offers us another cushion and a blanket with the title track. It makes me think of the painful awkwardness of seeing a long lost best friend and not quite knowing what to do with each other. You both desperately want to recapture that easy, relaxed casualness that once existed between you, but somehow you don't quite know the way back. It's the only track not to feature a piano, but has the most elaborate production on the EP with Riddell's vocals joined by guitar, bass, and violin. The bass performance is supplied by Kim Grant of [Raveloe](https://raveloemusic.bandcamp.com/) and [Dovetailed](/tags/dovetailed/) fame, and the violin comes from BBC Young Traditional Musician of the Year finalist [Madeleine Stewart](https://www.madeleinestewart.co.uk/).

*All Away Again* makes me think that those two friends have failed to reconnect and now just want to leave the situation and get back to reality (whatever that is). The simple arrangement of piano and voice is uplifting, but with a little bit of longing behind it. Happy to evacuate to safety, but sad that the expedition didn't reach its goal. I felt there was also a counterpoint in the piano compared to the solo arrangement in *Instrumental*, as if one was answering the challenge of the other with sad (but accepting) resignation.

*Restless And Alone* features the only drums on the EP (supplied by Kev Dunne), suggesting that the meeting is over and it's time to return to the rhythm of everyday life. It's a number cast in shadow evoking a sense that life might not be interesting enough without taking some big swings. In a dark world, without the support we deserve, perhaps our only choice is to take our dissatisfaction and turn it into action. For me, *Restless and Alone* wants us to embrace the chaos and see where the chips fall. What do we have to lose by trying in a world so stacked against us?

*Throwback To Another Dream* is an interesting EP. It's dark and brooding in many places but still suggests hope and embrace: not in an ethereal way, but with both hands grabbing on and shaking the dust off. It wants us to seek action and look for the good things that still remain. When I listen to this project, I hear a call to emphasise the good things in life, without ignoring the bad. With all that said, the music is beautiful and calming, and (at least to me) hopeful.

It's been a pleasure to listen to another great collection from Jason Riddell, and I very much look forward to the next. In the meantime, I have *Some Grief* and *Throwback To Another Dream* to keep me company.

[Throwback To Another Dream](https://jasonriddell.bandcamp.com/album/throwback-to-another-dream-ep) is available now.
